# Desk Cat

[Dashboard asset](desk-cat.png), made with the built-in image generation tool and resized for the app.

Prompt:

> Original anime mascot sticker for the Daytabs planner dashboard. A goofy squat apricot-orange cartoon cat with a lightning-shaped forelock and one oversized expressive squint, clutching a lilac retro pocket planner with three checkmarks, as if determined to finish a boss battle of chores. A couple tiny starbursts close to the silhouette, one bright orange-red. 90s hand-inked anime cel, bold almost-black aubergine contour, 3 or 4 flat color fills (sunflower yellow, tangerine, lavender, milk white), offset flat shadows, simple forms and big readable eyes. Asymmetrical gesture, full character, compact square composition, centered and takes up most of the canvas with small margins. No ground, no background, no typography, no extra props, no gradients. It should read at 100 pixels high. Transparent alpha background.

Three variants use the original cat as an image reference. Each uses this prompt with a different scene.

> Make one new standalone transparent PNG avatar to join Daytabs' original orange anime desk cat shown in the reference. Use the reference only for character continuity and bold sticker aesthetic. Scene: {scene}. Maintain recognizable orange-and-cream tabby fur, round paws, expressive face and tiny body. Dark purple bold outlines, flat tangerine, cream, yellow and lilac fills. Center one legible compact full silhouette, occupying roughly 85% of a square. It should work at 100 px. No text, lettering, border, scenery, floor shadow, extra characters or white background. The entire area outside the character and small accents must be transparent.

- [Coffee cat](desk-cat-coffee.png) — the orange cat wears round lavender glasses and hugs a comically oversized ivory coffee mug, with a little steam curl; relaxed closed-eye grin and thick curved tail; no lightning on forehead
- [Focus cat](desk-cat-focus.png) — the orange cat in an oversized violet headband is leaning forward and writing vigorously with a chunky sunflower-yellow pencil in an open white planner; determined eyes and small star accent; no lightning on forehead
- [Victory cat](desk-cat-victory.png) — the orange cat is doing a victory jump, paws raised, wearing a short lavender cape and holding a small gold star trophy, eyes squeezed closed in joy; two tiny yellow confetti accents; no lightning on forehead
